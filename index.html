Her style

Habits that show up again and again

Sauce piped in lines across the top

Mayo on the poke bowls, sour cream on the chili and taco salads. She finishes a lot of dishes with a sauce zig-zagged across the top.

Scallions to finish

Scattered over poke bowls, salmon and rice, loaded potatoes and egg dishes.

Avocado on a lot of things

Toast, bowls, tacos, chili, burgers, seared tuna.

Shredded parmesan on pasta

Almost every pasta gets a pile of shredded or shaved parmesan, and garlic toast often comes on the side.

Bowls, with toppings

A base (rice, lettuce or grains), a protein, then layers of toppings. This is the most common shape of meal across all two years.

Runny eggs

Fried or jammy eggs on toast, hash browns, English muffins and avocado.
