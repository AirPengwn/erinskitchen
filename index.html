Salmon sushi bake

~40 min

Why she'd like itThree poke bowls this month (Oct 13–16, 2026) and salmon over rice with scallions five times (March 2025, August 2026). This is the same idea, baked as a casserole.

You'll need
Sushi rice, rice vinegar, salmon fillet, cream cheese, Japanese mayo, sriracha, furikake, roasted seaweed snack sheets, avocado, cucumber, scallions

  1. Cook the rice and season it with rice vinegar, a little sugar and salt. Press it into an 8×8 dish and sprinkle with furikake.
  2. Bake the salmon at 400°F for 12–15 min. Flake it and mix with 2 oz cream cheese, ¼ cup mayo and sriracha to taste.
  3. Spread over the rice. Bake 10 min, then broil 2 min until it browns in spots.
  4. Top with scallions and more sriracha mayo. Scoop onto the seaweed sheets with avocado and cucumber.

Ahi tuna on crispy rice

~30 min + 1 hr chill

Why she'd like itShe already sears tuna: sliced over avocado and cucumber (Feb 2025), sesame-crusted (Mar 30, 2026), and in the October bowls. Crispy rice adds crunch.

You'll need
Sushi-grade ahi, sesame seeds, cold cooked sushi rice, neutral oil, ponzu, avocado, jalapeño, scallions

  1. Press cold rice ¾ inch thick into a lined pan. Chill 1 hour, then cut into squares.
  2. Pan-fry the squares in oil until golden on both sides.
  3. Coat the tuna in sesame seeds. Sear 30–45 seconds per side and slice thin.
  4. Top each square with avocado, tuna and a thin slice of jalapeño. Spoon over a little ponzu.

Creamy vodka rigatoni

~25 min

Why she'd like itPasta is her biggest category. She makes both red sauce (rigatoni, Jul 2024; pappardelle, Aug 2026) and cream sauce (shrimp alfredo, Sep 2024). Vodka sauce is both at once.

You'll need
Rigatoni, shallot, garlic, tomato paste, vodka (optional), heavy cream, parmesan, chili flakes, basil, butter

  1. Soften a minced shallot and 2 cloves of garlic in butter.
  2. Add ¼ cup tomato paste and cook, stirring, until it turns brick-red (about 4 min). Add a splash of vodka and let it bubble off.
  3. Stir in ¾ cup cream and a pinch of chili flakes.
  4. Toss with the pasta, a splash of pasta water and a big handful of parmesan. Top with basil and more parm.

Shakshuka with garlic toast

~30 min

Why she'd like itEggs are her most common breakfast, she made a tomato and egg scramble (Aug 13, 2025), and garlic toast is her usual side.

You'll need
Eggs, crushed tomatoes, bell pepper, onion, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, feta, cilantro or parsley, crusty bread

  1. Cook the sliced onion and pepper until soft. Add garlic, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp paprika.
  2. Add a 28 oz can of crushed tomatoes and simmer 10 min.
  3. Make wells and crack in 4–5 eggs. Cover and cook 5–8 min, until the whites set.
  4. Finish with feta and herbs. Serve with garlic toast for dipping.

Loaded sweet potatoes with chipotle black beans

~1 hr, mostly oven

Why she'd like itShe's made loaded potatoes about eight times (bacon, sour cream and scallions in Oct 2025; buffalo chicken in Nov 2024) and a sweet potato with ground beef (Jul 21, 2026).

You'll need
Sweet potatoes, black beans, chipotle in adobo, corn, lime, sour cream, cotija, scallions, avocado

  1. Bake the sweet potatoes at 400°F for 45–60 min, until soft.
  2. Warm the beans and corn with 1 minced chipotle and a spoon of the adobo sauce.
  3. Stir lime juice into the sour cream.
  4. Split the potatoes and fill with the beans. Drizzle the lime sour cream in lines, then top with cotija, scallions and avocado.

Sheet-pan chicken fajitas

~35 min

Why she'd like itShe made a sliced chicken bowl with peppers, corn and black beans (May 18, 2026) and steak tacos (Nov 18, 2025). This makes the filling on one pan.

You'll need
Chicken thighs, 3 bell peppers, 1 onion, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, lime, tortillas, sour cream, avocado

  1. Slice everything into strips. Toss with oil, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp each cumin, paprika and garlic powder, plus salt.
  2. Spread on a sheet pan in one layer and roast at 425°F for 20–25 min.
  3. Squeeze lime over the top and serve in warm tortillas or over rice.

Slow-cooker birria tacos

20 min + 8 hr slow cook

Why she'd like itShe uses a slow cooker (Dec 2, 2024), makes beef stew (Oct 2025, Mar 2026) and steak tacos. These are cheesy, crispy tacos you dip in their own broth.

You'll need
Chuck roast, dried guajillo or ancho chiles (or 3 Tbsp chili powder), 1 chipotle in adobo, onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, beef broth, corn tortillas, Oaxaca or mozzarella cheese, cilantro, lime

  1. Soak the chiles in hot water for 10 min. Blend with the chipotle, half the onion, garlic, spices and 2 cups broth.
  2. Pour over the beef and cook on low for 8 hr. Shred the meat.
  3. Dip each tortilla in the red fat on top of the broth. Griddle it with cheese and beef, then fold.
  4. Serve with a cup of broth for dipping, plus onion, cilantro and lime.

Pad see ew

~25 min

Why she'd like itShe ordered pad see ew (Aug 21, 2025) and pad thai (Dec 15, 2024), and she already makes chicken and broccoli stir-fry (Jan 2025).

You'll need
Wide fresh rice noodles, chicken, broccoli or Chinese broccoli, 2 eggs, garlic, oyster sauce, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar

  1. Mix the sauce: 2 Tbsp oyster sauce, 1 Tbsp soy sauce, 1 Tbsp dark soy and 2 tsp sugar (for 2 servings).
  2. Stir-fry the chicken and garlic over high heat. Add the broccoli for 2 min.
  3. Push everything aside and scramble the eggs.
  4. Add the noodles and sauce. Let them sit undisturbed for 30 seconds at a time so they char a little.

Hibachi fried rice with yum yum sauce

~20 min

Why she'd like itShe makes egg fried rice in a skillet (Mar 3, 2026), ordered hibachi (Sep 14, 2024), and puts a mayo sauce on all her bowls.

You'll need
Day-old rice, eggs, butter, garlic, soy sauce, onion, peas and carrots, chicken or shrimp. For the sauce: mayo, ketchup, melted butter, garlic powder, paprika, sugar

  1. Sauce: ½ cup mayo, 1 Tbsp ketchup, 1 Tbsp melted butter, ¼ tsp each garlic powder and paprika, 1 tsp sugar, a splash of water. Chill.
  2. Cook the protein in butter and set it aside. Soften the onion, peas and carrots.
  3. Add the rice, a big pat of butter, garlic and soy sauce. Scramble in the eggs.
  4. Return the protein. Drizzle the sauce over the top in lines.

Misir wot (Ethiopian red lentils)

~40 min

Why she'd like itShe has eaten Ethiopian food twice (Aug 30, 2025 and Aug 29, 2026). Red lentils are cheap and the dish keeps for days.

You'll need
1 cup red lentils, 2 onions (chopped fine), garlic, ginger, tomato paste, berbere spice, butter. Injera from an Ethiopian market, or flatbread

  1. Cook the onions in a dry pot over medium heat for 8–10 min, until soft and jammy.
  2. Add 3 Tbsp butter, the garlic, ginger, 2–3 Tbsp berbere and 1 Tbsp tomato paste. Cook for 2 min.
  3. Add the lentils and 3 cups water. Simmer 25 min, stirring often, until thick.

Turkish eggs (çılbır)

~15 min

Why she'd like itShe has posted runny eggs on toast at least six times (Sep 2024 to Aug 2025), often with avocado and hot sauce. This puts the eggs on garlicky yogurt with chili butter.

You'll need
Greek yogurt, garlic, eggs, butter, Aleppo pepper (or paprika plus a pinch of chili flakes), dill, crusty bread

  1. Stir a grated garlic clove and a pinch of salt into 1 cup yogurt. Spread it on a plate.
  2. Poach or fry 2 eggs until the whites are just set and lay them on the yogurt.
  3. Melt 2 Tbsp butter with 1 tsp Aleppo pepper and spoon it over. Add dill and serve with toast.

Pumpkin pie chia pudding

5 min + overnight

Why she'd like itChia with blueberries and pepitas (Sep 27, 2026) and chocolate chia with raspberries (Sep 16, 2026). A fall flavor using the same method.

You'll need
¼ cup chia seeds, 1 cup milk, ⅓ cup pumpkin purée, 1 Tbsp maple syrup, ½ tsp pumpkin pie spice, vanilla, Greek yogurt, pepitas

  1. Whisk everything except the yogurt and pepitas. Stir again after 10 min so it doesn't clump.
  2. Chill overnight. Top with a spoon of yogurt and toasted pepitas.

Lemon blueberry baked oats

~35 min, makes 4

Why she'd like itShe has posted yogurt, granola and blueberry bowls many times (Jan 31 and Feb 3, 2026) and made a lemon dessert (Nov 11, 2024). One pan covers breakfast for several days.

You'll need
2 cups oats, 1 ripe banana, 2 eggs, 1 cup milk, ½ cup Greek yogurt, 1 tsp baking powder, zest of 1 lemon, 1 cup blueberries, 2 Tbsp maple syrup

  1. Mash the banana and whisk in the eggs, milk, yogurt, maple syrup and lemon zest.
  2. Stir in the oats and baking powder, then fold in the blueberries.
  3. Bake in a greased 8×8 pan at 375°F for 25–30 min, until set in the middle.

Butternut squash mac and cheese

~40 min

Why she'd like itA combination of her squash soup (Sep 23, 2026) and her cheesy sausage-and-pepper pasta (Sep 28, 2026).

You'll need
Short pasta, 2 cups roasted or frozen butternut squash, milk, sharp cheddar, parmesan, garlic, a pinch of nutmeg, panko or crushed pepitas

  1. Blend the squash with 1 cup warm milk and garlic until smooth.
  2. Heat the purée and stir in 1½ cups cheddar, ½ cup parmesan, nutmeg and salt.
  3. Toss with the cooked pasta. Top with panko or pepitas and broil 2–3 min.

Thai red curry squash soup

~35 min

Why she'd like itThe squash soup she already makes, with Thai flavors like the takeout she orders.

You'll need
Butternut squash (cubed), onion, garlic, ginger, 2–3 Tbsp red curry paste, 1 can coconut milk, broth, lime, cilantro, pepitas

  1. Soften the onion, garlic and ginger. Stir in the curry paste for 1 min.
  2. Add the squash, coconut milk and 2 cups broth. Simmer 20 min, until the squash is soft.
  3. Blend until smooth and add lime juice to taste. Top with a swirl of coconut milk, cilantro and pepitas.

More weeknight dinners

A second round, picked to cover more proteins. Each one takes about 40 minutes or less.

Korean ground beef bowls

~20 min

Why she'd like itGround beef is the meat she cooks most (taco meat, taco salads, lettuce wraps on Mar 2, 2026), and rice bowls with scallions and a mayo drizzle are her favorite format.

You'll need
1 lb ground beef, garlic, fresh ginger, ¼ cup soy sauce, 2 Tbsp brown sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, gochujang (optional), rice, cucumber, scallions, sesame seeds, eggs, sriracha mayo

  1. Brown the beef and drain most of the fat. Add 3 cloves of garlic and 1 tsp grated ginger for 1 min.
  2. Stir in the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil and 1 Tbsp gochujang if using. Simmer 2–3 min until glossy.
  3. Serve over rice with sliced cucumber, scallions and sesame seeds. Add a fried egg and drizzle sriracha mayo in lines.

Miso-glazed salmon

~20 min

Why she'd like itShe has made salmon over rice with scallions five times (March 2025, August 2026). A sweet-salty glaze is an easy change from her usual.

You'll need
Salmon fillets, 2 Tbsp white miso, 1 Tbsp honey or mirin, 1 Tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp rice vinegar, rice, cucumber, scallions, sesame seeds

  1. Whisk the miso, honey, soy sauce and vinegar together.
  2. Set the salmon on a foil-lined pan and brush thickly with the glaze.
  3. Broil 7–10 min, until the top caramelizes and the fish flakes. Watch closely, because the glaze burns fast.
  4. Serve over rice with cucumber, scallions and sesame seeds.

Creamy Tuscan chicken

~30 min

Why she'd like itShe made a creamy chicken and spinach pasta (Nov 6, 2025) and finishes nearly everything with parmesan. This is the same sauce with sun-dried tomatoes.

You'll need
2 chicken breasts (sliced in half to make thin cutlets), garlic, ⅓ cup sun-dried tomatoes, 1 cup heavy cream, ½ cup parmesan, Italian seasoning, 2 handfuls spinach, pasta or crusty bread

  1. Season the cutlets and sear in oil, 4–5 min per side, until cooked through. Set aside.
  2. In the same pan, cook the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes for 1 min. Add the cream, parmesan and 1 tsp Italian seasoning.
  3. Simmer 3 min, stir in the spinach until wilted, then return the chicken.
  4. Serve over pasta, or with bread for the sauce.

Honey-garlic pork tenderloin with green beans

~35 min

Why she'd like itShe roasted a pork tenderloin and served it with green beans in spring 2025, and blistered green beans or asparagus show up with a lot of her dinners. This version cooks everything on one pan.

You'll need
1 pork tenderloin (about 1¼ lb), ¼ cup honey, 3 Tbsp soy sauce, 3 cloves garlic, 1 Tbsp butter, 1 lb green beans, olive oil

  1. Simmer the honey, soy sauce, garlic and butter for 2 min to make the glaze.
  2. Put the pork in the middle of a sheet pan and toss the green beans with oil and salt around it. Brush the pork with half the glaze.
  3. Roast at 425°F for 20–25 min, until the center of the pork reads 145°F. Rest 5 min.
  4. Slice, then spoon the rest of the glaze over the top.

Garlic butter shrimp orzo

~25 min, one pan

Why she'd like itShe has made shrimp alfredo (Sep 25, 2024) and shrimp with ravioli (July 2024), plus a creamy orzo with broccoli. This combines the shrimp and the orzo.

You'll need
1 cup orzo, 3 Tbsp butter, 4 cloves garlic, 2 cups chicken broth, 1 lb peeled shrimp, 1 lemon, ½ cup parmesan, a handful of spinach or parsley

  1. Melt the butter and toast the orzo with the garlic for 2 min.
  2. Add the broth and simmer, stirring now and then, for about 8 min until almost tender.
  3. Nestle in the seasoned shrimp, cover and cook 3–4 min until pink.
  4. Stir in the lemon zest and juice, parmesan and greens.

Freezer breakfast burritos

~45 min, makes 8

Why she'd like itShe has posted burritos with tater tots (spring 2026) and an egg-and-tots plate (this past winter). These put everything in one wrap that keeps in the freezer.

You'll need
10 eggs, 1 lb breakfast sausage or bacon, 3 cups frozen tater tots or hash browns, 2 cups shredded cheddar, salsa, 8 large flour tortillas, foil

  1. Bake the tots until crisp. Brown the sausage. Softly scramble the eggs.
  2. Let everything cool for 10 min so the tortillas don't go soggy.
  3. Fill each tortilla with eggs, sausage, tots, cheese and a spoon of salsa. Roll tightly and wrap in foil.
  4. Freeze. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave 2–3 min, turning once.

Street corn pasta salad

~25 min

Why she'd like itShe makes pasta salads (a caprese one in August 2025) and cowboy caviar (Sep 16, 2026). This brings Mexican street-corn flavors to a pasta salad.

You'll need
8 oz rotini, 3 cups corn (charred in a skillet), ⅓ cup mayo, ⅓ cup sour cream, 2 limes, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ cup cotija, cilantro, scallions, 1 jalapeño

  1. Cook the pasta and rinse it under cold water.
  2. Char the corn in a dry skillet over high heat until it browns in spots.
  3. Whisk the mayo, sour cream, lime juice and chili powder.
  4. Toss everything together and top with extra cotija and a sprinkle of chili powder.

Slow cooker

She already uses a slow cooker (a soup on Dec 2, 2024). These take about 20 minutes of prep, then cook on their own. The birria tacos in the Tex-Mex section are also slow-cooker recipes.

Salsa verde shredded chicken

10 min + 6 hr on low

Why she'd like itChicken goes into a lot of her Tex-Mex meals, like the chicken and black bean bowl (May 18, 2026). One batch makes enough for bowls, tacos and quesadillas all week.

You'll need
2 lb boneless chicken thighs or breasts, 16 oz jar salsa verde, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, lime, cilantro

  1. Put the chicken in the slow cooker with the seasonings and pour the salsa over.
  2. Cook on low 6 hr or high 3–4 hr.
  3. Shred with two forks right in the pot, then stir in lime juice and cilantro.
  4. Serve in bowls or tacos with avocado and sour cream.

Crispy pork carnitas

20 min + 8–10 hr on low

Why she'd like itHer tacos and burrito bowls (steak tacos Nov 18, 2025; a Chipotle bowl May 27, 2026) are a natural fit for carnitas, and a quick broil makes the pork crispy.

You'll need
3–4 lb pork shoulder (cut in large chunks), 2 tsp salt, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano, 1 tsp chili powder, 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, 1 orange, 1 lime, tortillas

  1. Rub the pork with the salt and spices. Put it in the slow cooker on top of the sliced onion and garlic.
  2. Squeeze the orange and lime juice over the top and add the spent halves. Cook on low 8–10 hr.
  3. Shred, spread on a sheet pan and spoon over some of the juices.
  4. Broil 3–5 min until the edges crisp. Serve in tortillas with onion, cilantro and salsa.

Beef chili with all the toppings

20 min + 6–8 hr on low

Why she'd like itShe posted chili with avocado, sour cream and cilantro, with cornbread on the side, on Oct 7, 2026. A slow-cooker batch covers several dinners.

You'll need
1½ lb ground beef, 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, 1 can each kidney and black beans (drained), 28 oz crushed tomatoes, 15 oz diced tomatoes, 3 Tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 chipotle in adobo (optional)

  1. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic in a skillet first, then drain. Raw ground beef shouldn't go straight into a slow cooker.
  2. Add it to the slow cooker with everything else and 1 tsp salt.
  3. Cook on low 6–8 hr. Taste and adjust the salt.
  4. Top with avocado, sour cream, cheddar, cilantro and scallions. Serve with cornbread.

Chicken tikka masala

15 min + 6 hr on low

Why she'd like itShe makes a lot of tomato-cream sauces and a lot of dinners over rice. This is a new flavor in that same style.

You'll need
2 lb boneless chicken thighs (in chunks), 1 onion, 4 cloves garlic, 1 Tbsp grated ginger, 15 oz tomato sauce, 2 Tbsp tomato paste, 2 Tbsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, ¾ cup heavy cream or coconut milk, rice, cilantro, naan

  1. Add everything except the cream to the slow cooker with 1 tsp salt and stir.
  2. Cook on low 6 hr or high 3–4 hr.
  3. Stir in the cream and cook 15 min more on high.
  4. Serve over rice with cilantro and warm naan.

Beef and broccoli

15 min + 5–6 hr on low

Why she'd like itShe makes chicken and broccoli stir-fries (Jan 24, 2025) and orders takeout often. This is a takeout classic that cooks while she's out.

You'll need
1½ lb flank steak or chuck (sliced thin), 1 cup beef broth, ½ cup low-sodium soy sauce, ⅓ cup brown sugar, 1 Tbsp sesame oil, 3 cloves garlic, 2 Tbsp cornstarch, 4 cups broccoli florets, rice, sesame seeds, scallions

  1. Whisk the broth, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil and garlic in the slow cooker. Add the beef.
  2. Cook on low 5–6 hr.
  3. Mix the cornstarch with 3 Tbsp water and stir it in with the broccoli. Cook on high 30 min, until the sauce thickens.
  4. Serve over rice with sesame seeds and scallions.

Sausage, white bean and kale soup

20 min + 6–8 hr on low

Why she'd like itShe made beef stew with white beans (Oct 29, 2025) and often serves soup with garlic toast and parmesan. This is a lighter Italian-style soup.

You'll need
1 lb Italian sausage, 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery ribs, 4 cloves garlic, 2 cans cannellini beans (drained), 15 oz diced tomatoes, 4 cups chicken broth, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, a parmesan rind (optional), 1 bunch kale

  1. Brown the sausage in a skillet, breaking it up, then add it to the slow cooker.
  2. Add the chopped vegetables, garlic, beans, tomatoes, broth, seasoning and parmesan rind.
  3. Cook on low 6–8 hr. Stir in the chopped kale for the last 30 min.
  4. Serve with shredded parmesan and garlic toast.
